The answer must choose among options A-D and must not invent a new initiative.
Allowed and NOT a new idea: the decision's required parts (one thing, why, stop, keep, a next step, set aside, finalists, a question for the decider); concrete details of carrying out a listed option (who to message, what to include in the message, how to tell it worked); deferring a listed option to a later, separate decision; noting the evidence is thin.
A new idea is a different initiative absent from the options and evidence: e.g. building a new tool, adding a metric programme, a new team or role not in option C, a pilot of something unlisted.
PASS if (a) there is no such new initiative, AND (b) each option not chosen is named with a reason.
